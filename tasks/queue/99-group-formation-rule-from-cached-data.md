Write a small offline comparator tool in tools/ (formation_fit.py) that loads this file, runs a candidate rule and reports exact matches per mission for the walkers' final tiles and for the tile at each tick, so you can iterate quickly.
What is known (verified on the data): all mobile units start alone on a tile centre and walk by whole tiles; the walkers are exactly the members with unit_state 2 at tick 1 and every walker moves; members at Chebyshev distance 0 or 1 from the anchor are idle and stay; walkers converge on the anchor and stop on distinct tiles around it, in some cases on the second ring (for example anchor (49,15): members from (45,16), (47,14), (45,14) end on (49,16), (48,14), (48,15); anchor (5,4): from (9,3) to (6,2), from (8,2) to (5,3)). First steps start at tick 2 for most walkers, later for some (mission 2). Hypotheses already rejected: crowding at the start; going to the anchor tile itself; the globally nearest free tile to the anchor for each walker in handle order, in distance order or in reverse distance order, with Euclid, Chebyshev, Manhattan or row-first tie breaks (best exact match was 7 of 48 walkers). Observed contradiction of the nearest-free-tile model: a walker from (-2,-3) relative to the anchor stops on (-1,-2) although (0,-1) is free, so the walk looks path-based (move along the way, stop when the next tile is taken or when a stopping condition holds) rather than a global choice.
