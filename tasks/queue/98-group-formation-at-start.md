Task: group formation at mission start (the real rule behind the early movement divergence of tasks 95 and 83).
Live evidence (2026-11-07, shared L0 runner, current develop, ticks 1 to 24, 30, 36, 48, 60, missions 1 to 3): tick 1 matches the original exactly (63, 104 and 171 objects). Afterwards the original moves 33 units in mission 1 (31 start walking at tick 2, 2 at tick 3), 9 in mission 3 and many in mission 2, while the clone moves none in missions 1 and 3. What was established with the new analysis:
1. It is not crowding: at tick 1 every mobile unit stands alone on its tile (60, 62 and 137 units on 60, 62 and 137 distinct tiles) and every unit stands exactly on a tile centre; after 60 ticks the missions 1 and 3 units are again exactly on tile centres, so the units move by whole tiles (most common displacements in tiles: (0,-1), (1,0), (0,1), (1,1), (-1,0), (-1,1), (3,2)).
2. The walkers are exactly the units that have unit_state 2 and execution_kind "walk" at tick 1 (31 of 60 in mission 1, 6 of 137 in mission 3, 11 of 62 in mission 2); all of them move; the idle ones stay (27 of 29 in mission 1).
3. The first step target (step_destination in pixels) is the centre of the tile one step along the way and equals the final tile for single-step units (for example tile (63,24) with anchor (64,26) steps to (63,25); tile (17,3) with anchor (17,5) steps to (18,4)). Longer walks continue for several tiles.
4. They do not land on the anchor tile (final tile differs from anchor_tile for all 31 and all 6 walkers) but gather in its neighbourhood on distinct tiles: three units from tiles (45,16), (47,14) and (45,14) sharing the anchor (49,15) end on (49,16), (48,14) and (48,15). This looks like formation slots around the tactic anchor, filled from the units' starting positions.
Hypothesis to verify, not to assume: at mission start the tactic of a group assigns each unit that is not yet in a slot a formation position near the anchor (see the formation notes in docs/rules_movement.md and the tactic hook found through vtable slot +0x11c and +0x120 in docs/idle_tactic_walk.md) and the units walk there as ordinary orders; units already in a slot stay idle.
